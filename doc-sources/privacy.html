Privacy Policy – SuperSafe Wallet

Effective date: July 29, 2025
Last updated: July 29, 2025

1. Introduction

SuperSafe Wallet is a non-custodial browser extension that enables users to interact with Ethereum-compatible blockchains. We value your privacy and are committed to handling your data responsibly.

2. Information We Collect

SuperSafe Wallet does not collect or transmit any personal or financial data to external servers.

However, in order to provide core wallet functionality, the extension:

  • Temporarily stores your encrypted private key in your browser’s local storage.
  • Displays on-chain information such as token balances, transaction history, and contract activity, retrieved directly from public blockchain nodes or third-party APIs.
  • Uses chrome.storage APIs to persist session state locally, never remotely.

3. How We Use This Information

The data stored is used solely to allow you to:

  • Sign blockchain transactions (e.g., swaps, token transfers)
  • Connect securely to decentralized applications (dApps)
  • Manage your wallet state across sessions

We do not:

  • Sell, rent or share your information with third parties
  • Use your data for advertising, analytics, or profiling

4. Data Storage and Security

All private data (including the encrypted private key and session token) is stored locally in your browser using secure storage APIs.

Encryption follows industry best practices, including:

  • AES-GCM encryption
  • PBKDF2 key derivation
  • Password timeout/session expiration

No information is sent to any centralized server controlled by us.

5. Your Control

Because SuperSafe Wallet is fully non-custodial:

  • You are the only one with access to your wallet
  • You may delete or reset your wallet at any time
  • If you uninstall the extension, all data is erased from your device
